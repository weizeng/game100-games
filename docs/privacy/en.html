Game100 Privacy Policy

Effective date: [TO FILL: effective date, e.g. 2026-11-01] · Last updated: 2026-10-20

This policy applies to the Android app “Game100” (游戏100, package com.game100.center, the “App”), provided by [TO FILL: developer name] (country/region: [TO FILL: developer country/region], “we”). The App is a collection of more than a hundred offline-playable HTML5 mini games that run inside the App's built-in WebView.

1. Summary

The App requires no account or sign-in. It does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos or device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.), and it contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or third-party login SDKs. Your game data stays on your own device.

2. Data stored on your device

The following data is kept only in the App's private storage on your device and is never uploaded to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App or using “Clear data” in system settings deletes all of it.

3. Network access

  • China app-store version: fully offline. All games are bundled; the App has no network permission and makes no network requests.
  • Google Play version and website version: to get new games, the App downloads the game catalog (manifest.json) and game files from these public content-delivery servers: cdn.jsdelivr.net, fastly.jsdelivr.net, raw.githubusercontent.com. The website version also reads the latest-version info (app.json) from the same servers and downloads new installers.

These requests carry no account, device identifier or personal information — only the requested file address and the app identifier Game100/<version> (User-Agent). As with visiting any website, the operators of these servers (jsDelivr and its CDN partners Cloudflare / Fastly, and GitHub) may log your IP address and request time for their own operation and security, under their own privacy policies: jsDelivr, GitHub. We have no access to those logs.

4. “Report bug” feature

Tapping “Report bug” on a game opens GitHub's “New issue” page in your phone's browser, pre-filled with the game ID, the App version and the description you typed. The App itself sends nothing. Only if you sign in to GitHub in the browser and choose to submit will that content be published publicly on GitHub, governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement. Please do not include personal information.

The App does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage, phone or other sensitive permissions.

6. Children's privacy

The App's content is suitable for all ages and some games are aimed at children. Because we collect no personal information and the App contains no ads or third-party tracking, no personal information about children is collected by us. We recommend that children under 13 (or the age set by your local law) use features that open external web pages, such as “Report bug”, with a parent or guardian.

7. Sharing, sale and international transfer

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Apart from the public CDN access logs described in section 3, there is no transfer of data to third parties or across borders.

8. Your rights

All data is on your device; you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or via Settings > Apps > Game100 > Storage > Clear data. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing to request from us for access, correction or deletion.

9. Changes

If the App's data practices change (for example, adding online features or third-party services), we will update this page and its “Last updated” date; significant changes will be announced in the App or in the store release notes.
